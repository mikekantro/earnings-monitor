What this is. An arithmetic decomposition: the index’s average change splits exactly into cohort contributions weighted by size. Membership requires management explicitly linking AI-infrastructure demand to the company’s own revenue, orders, backlog, or outlook, with a verbatim quote; no quote, no entry. Companies merely using AI internally are tracked on the AI Adoption tab, not here.

What this is not. Not a causal estimate. Second-order exposure (the freight carrier hauling transformers) is invisible unless stated, so the share is a floor on direct attribution. Self-attribution carries fashion risk: some companies may over-claim the theme, which the Q3 retest will measure. One check already runs against it: claimants without a stated figure improved -0.8, nearly matching the -0.0 of those with numbers, and both far exceed the -1.5 non-claimant baseline, so the cohort is not carried by numberless talk. Figures are equal-weighted; a capitalization-weighted cut would concentrate further.

Season note. Figures update with each refresh as late reporters post and are recomputed from the full matched sample each run.
