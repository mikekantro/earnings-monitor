How to read it. Scores are model judgments of pricing language on the call: realized pricing, announced increases, discounting, concessions, promotional intensity, and stated pass-through. 50 is the dividing line. Levels above 60 indicate broad, confident pricing; below 45, active concession.

What it is not. Not a CPI forecast and not audited pricing data. It is the corporate side of the inflation conversation, aggregated. Scores are comparable because the rubric and transcript window are fixed across all companies and both seasons.
